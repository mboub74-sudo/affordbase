## Source verification and calculations

Official source URLs are recorded in each guide's JSON and displayed to readers. Reviewed FCAC renewal, relief, card repayment, debt repayment, and counselling guidance; OSFI's uninsured straight-switch policy; and CRA opening, participation, deduction, withdrawal, closure, TFSA withdrawal, and HBP guidance.

Mortgage example: $350,000, 240 monthly payments, semi-annual nominal compounding converted to monthly. Rates are illustrative, not market quotes. At 5.50%, payment is $2,395.37; extending the same calculation to 300 payments gives $2,136.37.

Card example: $5,000, 19.99% annual rate divided by twelve, monthly interest then payment, no fees/new charges. $200: 33 payments and $1,521.02 interest. $300: 20 payments and $906.25 interest. $450: 13 payments and $575.96 interest. Final payments are smaller.

Savings example: $900 × 12 = $10,800; $8,000 plus $2,800 allocation. Illustrative 30% tax effect on $8,000 is $2,400, not a guaranteed refund. $53,000 target minus $20,000 saved leaves $33,000; divided by $900 is approximately 36.7 months.
