## Search and three-page reviews

### Canada Groceries and Essentials Benefit 2027
Query: Canada Groceries and Essentials Benefit 2027 eligibility payment supplement.
Reviewed: CRA overview, eligibility, and payment-amount pages. Budget 2026 chapter 3, tax annex, and government calendar were used to resolve proposal status and payment timing.
- https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/child-family-benefits/canada-groceries-essentials-benefit.html

- https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/child-family-benefits/canada-groceries-essentials-benefit/how-much/payment-amounts.html
Original contribution: separate payment streams, dated planning status, cash-flow examples and clarification that combined maxima are not a universal single cheque. Proposed supplement is not assigned an invented exact payment date or personal award.

### Why grocery prices do not fall when inflation falls
Query: why are grocery prices not going down if inflation is dropping Canada.
Reviewed:
- https://www.bankofcanada.ca/2026/02/sparks-at-bank-article-2026-3/
- https://www.bankofcanada.ca/2024/07/what-drives-up-the-price-of-groceries/
- https://publications.gc.ca/collections/collection_2026/parl/xc12-1/XC12-1-2-451-47-eng.pdf
Original contribution: compounded price-level example, personal spending diagnosis, package-size calculations, usable bulk quantities and a realistic weekly-to-monthly budget conversion. Dated statistics are not presented as permanent or universal price levels.

### Walmart price match policy Canada 2026
Query: Walmart price match policy Canada 2026.
Reviewed three relevant accessible consumer pages:
- https://www.walmart.ca/en/help/article/pricing-policy/0677cc5f171e42509e9dbf66ba09be7a
- https://pricegoose.ca/blog/does-walmart-price-match-canada/
- https://kacheng.ca/en/guides/supermarket-flyers-price-matching/
A seller-documentation result was screened out as consumer policy evidence; an inaccessible NabPick result was not treated as verified. Original contribution: policy distinctions, fallback shopping plan, original unit-price and basket examples, and separation of price matching from adjustments. Policy facts were verified against Walmart itself; competitor material was not copied.

The three new guides link to the existing cornerstone page rather than duplicating its primary intent.
