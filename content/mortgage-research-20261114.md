The two requested references combine affordability inputs with explanations of the major borrowing constraints. The new guides expand individual questions into reproducible examples: contract versus qualifying payment; balance at renewal; full versus underwriting condo fees; the gap before TDS becomes binding; and down-payment cash remaining after closing. Competitor text, images and numerical examples were not copied. Financial rules are attributed to official sources in each guide. Legacy or conflicting source statements were checked against current CMHC and OSFI program pages rather than repeated.

## Calculation method
Canadian fixed-rate examples use r = (1 + j/2)^(1/6) - 1, with monthly payment M = P*r/(1-(1+r)^(-n)). Lifetime figures explicitly hold the assumed rate constant; they are not forecasts. Mortgage capacity denotes the financed balance, not purchase price. Rates and property costs are hypothetical.
